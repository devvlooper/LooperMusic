## Important: our package name has changed

With respect to everyone who has been using Looper, we want to tell you this clearly: the app's Android package name has changed from `com.looper.player` to `com.looper.music`.

Android treats this as a new app, so this APK **installs alongside your current Looper instead of updating it**. Your library, settings and downloads do not move across on their own. Please keep your current app until you have everything you need in the new one.

We are sorry for the extra step, and thank you for your patience and for staying with Looper.

## What's New

### New
- Song sheet in Now Playing: tap the song title or artist to view the artist or album, read about the song, or check stream info
- Blur Music Bar: an optional translucent, blurred music bar, in Player Interface while Dynamic Theming is off

### Improved
- Now Playing opens and closes with a softer, smoother motion
- Now Playing backgrounds crossfade gently on every player style and background type
- Quality details now show format, decoder, resampler, DSP and output, and online songs report what is really playing
- Home and library refresh automatically once each morning, afternoon and evening. Pull to refresh still works anytime
